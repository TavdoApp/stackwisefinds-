# High-Converting Content Writer Skill

This skill defines the editorial standards, structure, tone, and conversion copy patterns for all articles published on StackwiseFinds.

---

## 1. Editorial Tone & Persona

- **Tone**: Pragmatic, direct, expert-level operator perspective. Zero buzzwords, zero generic corporate padding.
- **Goal**: Help the reader make a clear, confident buying decision in under 5 minutes.
- **Banned Phrases**:
  `in today's fast-paced digital world`, `game-changer`, `cutting-edge`, `seamless workflow`, `look no further`, `in conclusion`, `without further ado`.

---

## 2. Article Structure Standard (1400 – 2200 Words)

Every listicle or comparison guide MUST follow this exact structure:

```
[Title] Top [N] Best [Category] for [Target Persona] ([Year] Guide)
├── 1. Executive Summary & Quick Winners Box (Top Pick, Best Value, Best Budget)
├── 2. Buyer Decision Matrix (Comparison Table with specs & direct partner links)
├── 3. Deep-Dive Software Reviews
│   ├── Tool Name + Logo + Overall Rating (x/10)
│   ├── Direct Verdict ("Best for...")
│   ├── Key Features Bullet Points
│   ├── Pricing Breakdown (Free trial info, tier breakdown)
│   ├── Pros & Cons List
│   └── Direct Call-To-Action Button ("Visit [Tool] ↗")
├── 4. How to Choose (Buying Guide Criteria)
├── 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ Accordion with JSON-LD Schema)
└── 6. FTC Affiliate Disclosure & Editorial Integrity Statement
```

---

## 3. Monetization & Conversion Optimization

- **Outbound Links**: All affiliate links use tracked clean parameters (`https://tool.com?aff=stackwisefinds`).
- **Button Micro-Copy**: Use high-intent action verbs (*"Visit Site ↗"*, *"Claim Free Trial ↗"*, *"Check Live Pricing ↗"*).
- **Callout Highlight Boxes**: Highlight special deals or exclusive discounts in styled green quote boxes (`.article-highlight-box`).
